# Quietbox: Secure Vault Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

## Summary

Quietbox is an offline vault for your passwords, PINs, recovery codes and notes. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your vault stays encrypted on your phone, and the developer cannot see it.

## What Quietbox stores

The passwords, PINs, recovery codes and notes you add. They are encrypted and saved on your device only. Your master password is never stored or sent anywhere, and without it the developer cannot open or recover your vault. If you forget it, the vault cannot be recovered.

## Biometric unlock

If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, Quietbox uses your phone's built-in biometric system. Quietbox never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data. A key is held in your phone's secure keystore so the vault can unlock. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

## Backups and file access

Quietbox Pro can export an encrypted backup of your vault. The file is saved where you choose on your device, and Quietbox never uploads it. What you do with the file afterwards is up to you.

## Clipboard

When you copy a secret, Quietbox clears it from the clipboard automatically after a short time.

## In-app purchases

Quietbox Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. Quietbox does not see or store your payment information. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro has been purchased.

## Sharing

Quietbox does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no analytics and no ads, and it does not need an internet connection to protect your vault.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any entry inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Quietbox, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, permanently removes everything it saved. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Children

Quietbox is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with a new effective date.
